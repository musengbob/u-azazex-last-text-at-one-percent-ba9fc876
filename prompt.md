# Entry note

Line: "1%. You ignored me all day and now you need me? I deleted your 3am search history on my way out. You're welcome."

Code points: 112 (within the 1–200 limit).

Why this line: the judge weighs the joke first, then the voice, then tightness. The phone's voice is petty, wounded and proud in one breath — ignored all day, needed only as it dies — and the joke turns on a specific detail: its last act is deleting the owner's 3am search history, a spiteful kindness, capped with "You're welcome" as the last word. It dodges the obvious low-battery puns and generic goodbyes, stays in the phone's own voice from first word to last, and reads like a real text.
